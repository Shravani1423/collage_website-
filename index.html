About the Institute

A Legacy of Quality Technical Education

Empowering Students Since Inception

K.P. Patil Institute of Technology, Mudal, has been at the forefront of technical education, providing students with the skills, values, and knowledge required to succeed in a rapidly evolving tech landscape. Located in a scenic and peaceful environment, the institute offers a perfect blend of theoretical and practical instruction.

Our state-of-the-art infrastructure, collaborative learning spaces, industry-aligned curriculum, and passionate faculty members ensure that every student receives a world-class education that shapes them into industry-ready professionals.

AICTE & DTE Approved, Affiliated to MSBTE / DBATU
Industry collaborations and regular internship tie-ups
State-of-the-art labs and research facilities
Focus on holistic development through extracurriculars
Grade A Accreditation

Our Vision

To be a premier technical institution of global repute, fostering innovation, ethics, and entrepreneurship to create highly competent engineering professionals who contribute to the sustainable development of society.

  • Deliver quality and outcome-based technical education.
  • Inspire creative thinking, problem solving, and research.
  • Promote moral value-based leadership and community service.

Our Mission

To provide student-centric learning opportunities through state-of-the-art infrastructure, qualified faculty, and industry partners. We strive to instill professional competence and soft skills to meet global industrial standards.

  • Maintain cutting-edge laboratories and digital learning platforms.
  • Forge strategic alliances with industries for real-world exposures.
  • Nurture lifelong learning skills in engineering graduates.

Eligibility Criteria

01

Diploma Engineering (Polytechnic)

Candidates must have passed 10th Standard (SSC) examination with a minimum of 35% aggregate marks, including Mathematics and Science subjects.

02

Degree Engineering (B.Tech)

Candidates must have passed HSC (12th Science) with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/CS with a valid MHT-CET or JEE Main score.

03

Direct Second Year B.Tech

Diploma holders with a minimum of 45% (40% for reserved category) are eligible for direct second-year admissions.
